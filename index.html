Terms & Conditions

1. Devotional Decorum & Code of Conduct

Birla Dharamshala is a sacred guest sanctuary. Consumption of alcohol, tobacco, non-vegetarian food, and smoking is strictly prohibited inside the premises.

2. Check-In & Check-Out Times

Standard check-in is 12:00 PM and check-out is 11:00 AM. Early check-in is subject to availability of vacated and cleaned rooms.

3. Identity Verification

Valid government photo identification (Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID) is mandatory for every staying guest at the time of check-in.

4. Reservations & Confirmation

Reservation requests are subject to room availability and room manager confirmation. During major festivals like Ram Navami, Deepotsav, and Vivah Panchami, advance token confirmation may be required.

Privacy Policy

1. Information We Collect

We only collect basic guest information (Name, Contact Number, Dates of Stay, and Guest Count) for processing room reservations and coordinating your pilgrimage stay.

2. Use of Information

Your phone number and name are solely used for WhatsApp/SMS booking confirmations, check-in updates, and guest support during your stay in Ayodhya.

3. Data Security

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third parties. All guest records are kept securely.
